INTRODUCTION

Silent Expression is a one-shot visual novel inspired by a true story. It follows a hard-of-hearing protagonist named Egon who struggles to speak clearly and communicates through sign language. Lost in a new and unfamiliar town, they’re trying to find their way to a specific destination but they can’t do it alone.

Year: 2025

Genre: Slice of Life

Platform: PC / MAC

Engine: Ren'py

Development Time: 2 weeks
