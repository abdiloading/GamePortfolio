STORY OVERVIEW

Aeris Tennis is a Victorian-era steampunk visual novel about doubles tennis, teamwork, and trust.

The player is placed into a high-stakes doubles tournament where winning can lead to scholarships, sponsors, and a future as a professional athlete while losing could mean everything falls apart.

The key twist is that the team was never meant to work.

The player may have been selected on purpose by the Host, either as entertainment or as a social experiment, to see what happens when incompatible people are forced to cooperate. Alternatively, the team may have been assembled through sabotage, where an opposing team bribed officials or the host to create a group full of clashing personalities.

Either way, the MC is dropped into chaos and must survive it.
